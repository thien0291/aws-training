# AWS-authored ML Engineer Associate practice

Choose your exam version first. As of 2026-09-24, [AWS lists MLA-C01 as current and MLA-C02 as an updated beta](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-machine-learning-engineer-associate/). C01 English testing ends 2026-09-28; C02 beta delivery begins 2026-09-29. The beta expands GenAI, RAG, foundation-model, and agentic coverage. Recheck AWS's page before studying or booking.

1. On that [official certification page](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-machine-learning-engineer-associate/), open **Prepare for the exam** for your selected version and launch the **AWS Certification Official Practice Question Set** in Skill Builder. AWS's [general prep page](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certification-prep/) describes its free 20-question sets and subscription full-length Official Practice Exams.
2. Check missed questions against the appropriate [MLA-C01 guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/machine-learning-engineer-associate-01/) or [MLA-C02 beta guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/machine-learning-engineer-associate-02/).
3. Use [training-extract.md](training-extract.md) for fraud data preparation, inference, and introductory RAG items. Use dedicated ML training for model development and MLOps misses.

The [local practice test](practice-test.md) is original and course-focused, not AWS-authored. Official AWS practice questions remain in Skill Builder so version-specific feedback stays current.
